# Baltor Spatial Motion Composer

Use `README.md` and `AGENTS.md` as the source of truth. Input: a storyboard and original/authorized artwork. Output: layout-rich full MP4, composition report, JSON shots/graphs, and source provenance. Do not assume internet reference videos are available. The standalone `Baltor_Spatial_Studio_Editor.html` is for visual layout authoring; the Pillow renderer produces authoritative pixels. Required QA: tests, safe boxes, collision diagnostics, full video probe, and human visual approval. Never publish without approval.
