## Quantified project

- **15 scenes, 45 secondary timing cues**, 19 compositional DAG nodes.
- Independent motion categories: authored stick figure, camera, backgrounds/parallax, foreground occlusion, weather, punctuation FX, framing panels, sound.
- Original audio and graphics. No third-party clip processed in reference analysis.
- Goal of the 70-second clip: demonstrate a reusable edit grammar, not reproduce an existing video.
