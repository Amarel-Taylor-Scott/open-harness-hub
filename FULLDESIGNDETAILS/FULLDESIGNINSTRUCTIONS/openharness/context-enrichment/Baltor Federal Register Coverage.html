Regulatory coverage · CFR · Federal Register

Govern against the whole Federal Register — not a fixed list.

Baltor doesn’t memorize regulations. It pins each corpus to the live eCFR and watches the Federal Register for changes — so when a rule is amended, the affected context reconciles and re-verifies against the source in force. Any of the 50 CFR titles is addressable; the worked examples below are concrete instances. Not legal advice — Baltor preserves the citation and the receipt; the rule in force is the authority.
