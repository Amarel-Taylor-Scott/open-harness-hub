The weak spots, resolved or queued

Where your context is missing support, stale, or out of date with the source of truth, your agent has a blind spot. Baltor finds each weak spot, resolves what it can from authority, and queues the rest for follow-up research.

▼ weak spot — gap / stale / needs support ▲ resolved — reconciled against official source ◑ watched — temporally fragile, re-checked on a schedule
