Processing & Decomposition

A normalized source record is split into atomic_facts, each tagged with a claim_status and a ctx:// source handle. Narrative allegations are held out — kept, never erased, never served as fact. This page is a projection over one real pipeline run — it computes no truth, it reads /api/pipeline/decomposition.

idle
Lossless law: decomposition is not deletion. Every atomic fact points back to its source handle, and every held-out narrative allegation is retained beside the facts (shown below), tagged unverified_allegation — visible, attributable, and rehydratable, just not promotable to a served answer.
