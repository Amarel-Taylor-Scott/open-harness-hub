**PRIVATE-FIRST candidate hub** — built on the shared kit, kept private until a competitor
enters the lane. Open methods for pack shaping — summarize, structure, rank, and fit context to the task budget. One of the four **context-governance method hubs** that open Baltor's
engine stages (Reconcile · Harden · Enhance · Optimize) as registries of reusable methods.

## Status & release policy
- **status:** `private` (in `shared/products.js` → `PORTFOLIO.ENTITIES`). Muted accent +

  `status:'private' → 'live'` flip.

- discovery ≠ trust; never public without owner clearance.
