## The market in one paragraph (what changed in the last ~90 days)

The "prove it, don't just generate it" category got **two fresh funding proof points** — **Pramaana ($27M, Khosla,
2026-06-17, LEAN formal verification for tax/law/medicine)** and **Probably ($9M, a16z+Accel, ~2026-06-16,
weak-model-in-a-deterministic-workflow** — our model-downgrade thesis, *secondary-source amount, verify*). The
**format wars ended**: **SKILL.md** became the cross-vendor skill standard (Anthropic 2025-12; adopted by
Microsoft/OpenAI/~32 tools; donated to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation) and **Google's OKF** (2026-06-12)
is the context-format analog — so *format is no longer a moat; assurance is*. The **data-gravity giants are racing
the context layer but do not verify truth**: Databricks **Genie Ontology + Unity AI Gateway** (DAIS, 2026-06-16) and
Snowflake **Horizon Context** (2026-04-21) ship access-governance, lineage, and PII alerting — **not** truth/freshness
verification or portable cross-vendor receipts. And the **profession-by-profession generalization is being funded
in the open**: Harvey ($11B, 2026-03-25), Legora ($5.6B, 2026-04-30), Rogo ($160M, finance, 2026-04-29), Arctic
Health (YC, healthcare credentialing), maritime digital credentials (BIMCO charter, 2026-06-09), Exiger/Kharon
(forced-labor). Meanwhile **context cost collapsed** (free 1M-token windows, ~80% price drops) — which *closes
transient gaps fast* and raises the bar to **structural** lift.

## Strengths

- **The assurance layer is BUILT + proof-gated** (520+ deterministic proofs, preflight GO): verification + freshness/
  CDC + earned source-authority + portable receipts — the thing Pramaana/Probably do *narrowly* (one method/vertical)
  and the data-gravity platforms *don't do at all*.
- **Provider-neutral + open, riding ABOVE the stack** — we *consume* the winning open formats as candidates (OKF,
  FollowTheMoney, SKILL.md, OpenSharing) and add the assurance they lack. The format standardization is a tailwind,
  not a threat.
- **A durable-gap factory, now generalized**: the profession-scale seeder (15 professions / 11 sectors / 55 candidates)
  turns the validated "each profession has registries a base model can't enumerate" pattern into governed candidates,
  grounded in the **O*NET + WORKBank** spine — exactly WORKBank's "R&D-Opportunity" zone (high delegation-desire ×
  low current capability).
- **Governance discipline as a feature**: discovery≠trust, serves_truth=false, clean-room license guardrails — the
  curation the skill marketplaces lack (Vercel/Snyk now sell pre-install scanning; the industry just discovered our
  "raw → unique → verified → promoted" problem).
- **A real, regulated beachhead** (OFAC live-receipt + entity-intelligence) in a domain with **dated regulatory
  forcing functions** (EU Forced Labour Regulation module live 2026-05-17; OFAC "gatekeeper" enforcement expanding).

## Weaknesses

- **Not live on real cloud** (`go_live_ready=False`; the live seams are emulated locally). The funded competitors
  **ship**; we have an engine + emulators, not a deployed product.
- **Breadth is candidate-stage** — the profession seeds and thousands of candidates are *proposals*; lift is
  *demonstrated*, not yet a production-eval number. Discovery≠trust cuts both ways: nothing is promoted.
- **No capital, no distribution** vs Harvey ($11B) / Legora ($5.6B) / Databricks / Snowflake. We win on substrate,
  not spend.
- **Horizontal-vs-vertical exposure** — a generalist governed layer can be out-executed in any single vertical by the
  funded specialist (Harvey/legal, Rogo/finance, Arctic Health/credentialing). Going wide risks being shallow everywhere.
- **Single-operator throughput** — the surface is broad relative to who maintains it.

## Threats

- **Data-gravity giants extend from access-governance into truth-verification** (Databricks Genie Ontology + Unity AI
  Gateway; Snowflake Horizon Context). They own the data and the buyer; if they add CDC/verification, the white space
  narrows. **Closest land-grab to watch.**
- **Funded validators broaden** — Pramaana (formal proofs) or Probably could go from vertical/method-specific to a
  horizontal assurance plane.
- **Context-cost collapse closes transient gaps** (free 1M windows; ~71× cost spread; ~80% price drops). Mitigation:
  keep re-screening the corpus against current prices (`decay_signal`) so we only bank **structural** lift — which the
  profession-registry gap is (a model still has no live access to the authoritative source).
- **EU high-risk deadline likely slips to 2027-12-02** (Digital Omnibus political deal 2026-05-07, *not yet adopted*) —

- **Marketplace/skill commoditization + consolidation** (predicted 8→3-4 marketplaces) — but this is mostly a tailwind

## PMF read + the expansion thesis (DueCare → all professions)

**The white space is confirmed open by the incumbents' own moves**: everyone is racing to own *where context lives*
(OKF format, Genie Ontology, Horizon Context, UC brokering) — **nobody leads with whether it's TRUE and CURRENT,
provably, portably, above any one platform.** *"They standardize where context lives; Baltor governs whether it's true."*

**The owner's expansion thesis is market-validated, not speculative.** Healthcare credentialing, maritime credentials,
legal, finance, and forced-labor are each *independently* funding/regulating "this profession's registries base models
can't enumerate." DueCare (migrant recruitment) is **one instance** of a general, durable pattern; the profession-scale
seeder generalizes it across O*NET occupations. The right discovery spine is **O*NET + WORKBank**, not raw Wikipedia
scraping (rigorous, citable, ToS-clean, and WORKBank's R&D-Opportunity zone is already the negative-space map).

**The strategic line to hold:** generalize as **substrate**, not as a vertical competitor. Seed governed capabilities
*per profession* that the funded vertical players (and the data-gravity platforms) **consume**, and differentiate where
all of them are weakest — continuous verification, earned source-authority, CDC/currency, portable receipts.

1. **Go live on the compliance beachhead** (S0 hosting + S1 live source/CDC + receipts) — turn the emulated seams real
