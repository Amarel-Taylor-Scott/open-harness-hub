# UN SDG alignment-claim quality rubric v1

*rubric* · `rubric/sdg-alignment-v1` · v0.1.0 · experimental

Six-dimension rubric for grading the QUALITY of an SDG alignment
claim — whether for a corporate sustainability disclosure, NGO
program, government budget item, or impact-investment thesis.

Grades DISCLOSURE QUALITY, not SDG IMPACT. A program may genuinely
contribute to an SDG but score low on this rubric if the claim is
poorly substantiated; conversely, a high-quality claim may still
describe a marginal contribution.
