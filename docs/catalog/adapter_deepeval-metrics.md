# DeepEval metrics library (eval Actions source)

*adapter* · `adapter/deepeval-metrics` · v0.1.0 · experimental

Wraps Confident AI's DeepEval (YC W25, OSS) as a source of
candidate eval Actions: G-Eval, faithfulness, answer relevancy,
hallucination, RAGAS-style metrics — runnable as harness steps. LLM-judged
metrics are themselves model outputs: scores are recorded as candidate
signals with judge provenance, never as the promotion gate's truth —
deterministic gates own promotion.
WRAP CANDIDATE (discovery ≠ trust): admitted from the 2026-06 YC/tool
landscape research as a governed CANDIDATE behind a port — its output is
never served as truth, it is sandboxed, and measured lift is PENDING
(two-axis gate: lift AND structural durability) before any promotion.
