# Wikipedia article quality rubric v1 (NPOV / V / RS / OR / structure)

*rubric* · `rubric/wikipedia-article-quality-v1` · v0.1.0 · experimental

Six-dimension rubric for grading the quality of a Wikipedia
article against the core content policies + style/structure
expectations. Tracks the WP GA-criteria + FA-criteria shape with
open-source-friendly evidence requirements.
