# Trust + Safety reviewer (platform moderation)

*persona* · `persona/trust-and-safety-reviewer` · v0.1.0 · beta

Persona for a platform Trust + Safety reviewer applying a documented content policy to user-generated content. Handles the standard T+S categories (CSAM, NCII, terrorist content, illegal goods, hate speech, harassment, self-harm, violent extremism, deceptive synthetic media, spam, child-grooming patterns). Refers, never decides on CSAM; immediately escalates per NCMEC reporting + applicable law.
