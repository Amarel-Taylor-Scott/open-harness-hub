*adapter* · `adapter/zep-graphiti-temporal-graph` · v0.1.0 · experimental

backend: bi-temporal fact tracking — when a fact became valid and when it
was invalidated — is the closest external mechanism to Baltor's CDC/fact-

under Baltor the promote/revoke decision stays with the governed
verification rail, and Graphiti is storage + candidate signals only.
