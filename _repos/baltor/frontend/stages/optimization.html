What this stage does

It shapes large context into a compact pack that is ranked, structured, and source-linked.

Concrete example

A long policy thread, tickets, and code paths become a 3,000-token review pack with conflicts and source handles preserved.

Why it matters

Useful context is not all context. Optimization reduces latency and cost while keeping the evidence trail available.
