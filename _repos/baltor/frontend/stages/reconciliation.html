During reconciliation, Baltor actively looks for contradictions, weak source authority, stale evidence, ambiguous entity matches, and places where source precedence needs human review.

contradictions source precedence entity matching review queue
What this stage does

It deduplicates related objects, aligns entities across systems, compares claims, and makes disagreements visible instead of letting conflicting chunks enter a prompt.

Concrete example

Jira, a policy page, and code all describe the refund window differently. Baltor ranks evidence, applies source precedence, and flags the stale source when confidence is not enough.

Why it matters

AI workflows fail when conflicting context is silently summarized. Reconciliation turns disagreement into a structured decision or a reviewable exception.
