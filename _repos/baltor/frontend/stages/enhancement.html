What this stage does

It enriches reconciled objects with verified external facts, service relationships, owner context, tools, and metadata.

Concrete example

For a credit-card late-fee claim, Baltor attaches an approved external source or tool result before the pack uses the value.

Why it matters

Internal context is often incomplete. Enhancement adds missing context without turning every raw source into prompt bloat.
