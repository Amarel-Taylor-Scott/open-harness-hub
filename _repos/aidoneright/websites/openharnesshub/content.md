# OpenHarnessHub

> Open skills, harnesses, templates, rubrics, datasets, and eval packs for agentic infrastructure.

**Kind:** OpenHarnessHub · open ecosystem

**Audience:** Open-source contributors, builders, and evaluators of agentic infrastructure.

## What it is
- An open capability ecosystem: skills, harnesses, templates, rubrics, datasets, eval/provider packs.
- The neutral, OSS-first home of reusable evidence assets and the open CapabilityTask spec.

## What it is not
- Not a hosted runtime (Teleon runs capabilities).
- Not a hosted SaaS product.
- Not a source of trusted-by-default skills.

## Owns
- Skills
- Harnesses
- Templates
- Eval packs
- Capability packs
- Provider packs
- Rubrics
- Datasets

## Does not own
- Runtime execution
- Customer context truth
- Skill activation without evaluation

## Relationship
OpenHarnessHub supplies reusable capability parts. Teleon uses its templates/skills/evals; Baltor uses its context/decomposition/verification/compliance harnesses. It runs nothing itself.
