# AI Done Right

> Infrastructure for governed, self-improving AI systems.

**Kind:** Holding company · portfolio

**Audience:** Founders, investors, partners, and researchers evaluating the portfolio.

## What it is
- A holding company coordinating a portfolio of governed-AI infrastructure.
- The home of shared research, brand architecture, open standards, and IP coordination.

## What it is not
- Not a product you deploy.
- Not a runtime — it owns no runtime code.
- Not an owner of customer context truth or skill activation.

## Owns
- Portfolio strategy
- Shared research
- Brand architecture
- Open standards coordination
- IP coordination

## Does not own
- Runtime truth
- Customer context truth
- Skill activation

## Relationship
OpenHarnessHub supplies reusable capability parts. Teleon runs and evolves capabilities. Baltor governs the context they produce. AI Done Right coordinates the portfolio.
