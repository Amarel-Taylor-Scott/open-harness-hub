# INC-CFPB-07: dispute-resolution SLA breaches

- Severity: SEV-3
- Date: 2026-05-12
- Area: EFT dispute handling

## Summary

A cluster of complaints (incl. COMP-5521) shows disputes quoted a 30-day window, breaching the Reg E
10-business-day requirement. Root cause: agents following the stale disputes FAQ instead of Reg E.

## Follow-up

Reaffirm Reg E §1005.11 (10 business days; 45 with provisional credit) as the authority and correct
the FAQ.
