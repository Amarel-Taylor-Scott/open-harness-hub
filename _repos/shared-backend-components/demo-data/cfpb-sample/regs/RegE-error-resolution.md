# Reg E — EFT error resolution timeline (authoritative)

- Status: In force
- Authority: Regulation E (Electronic Fund Transfer Act), §1005.11
- Supersedes: the timeline in `docs/disputes-faq.md`

## Rule

On a timely notice of an error involving an electronic fund transfer, the institution must
investigate and resolve within **10 business days** of receiving the notice. The period may be
extended to **45 calendar days** if the institution provisionally credits the consumer's account.

> Authoritative value: **error_resolution_days = 10 business days** (extendable to 45 with
> provisional credit).

The internal disputes FAQ that still says "30 days" is superseded by this rule and must be treated
as stale until corrected.
