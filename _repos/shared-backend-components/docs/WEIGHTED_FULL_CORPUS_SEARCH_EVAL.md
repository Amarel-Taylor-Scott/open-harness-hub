## Success model (mode-specific)
- **curated_default:** highest precision on GENERAL queries (the precision-safe default)
- **weighted_diversity_full:** recovers useful candidates on curated-MISS / coverage-GAP / low-confidence queries without unacceptable bloat, and does not bury certified/verified hits under filters. Need NOT beat curated on general precision.
- **question_answered:** can the 1.15M tier DISCOVER useful missing candidates (later executor-certifiable)?

## Verdict

NET WIN + MODE-SPECIFIC WIN (no spin). OVERALL on the full eval, the naive full-corpus union (arm B) DIPS below the curated default (arm A) — P@1 0.668 vs 0.728 — reproducing the dip that started this task. Tier-weighting + the control pipeline + diversity (arm C) does NOT just fix the dip, it OVERTAKES curated overall: P@1 0.764 (> 0.728), MRR 0.846 (> 0.817), with LOWER generic pollution (0.010 vs 0.035). On the ORIGINAL 400-query head that first showed the dip, arm B falls to P@1 0.693 but arm C recovers to 0.708 (= curated 0.708) with better MRR/nDCG and less pollution — the dip was a ranking artifact, now corrected. DISCOVERY where curated is weakest: on CURATED-MISS queries (curated returns nothing relevant) arm C recovers 41.2% of them (56.2% with the certified-only filter); on CURATED-LOW-CONFIDENCE queries P@1 rises 0.399 -> 0.566 (certified-only 0.647). These are exactly the candidates an executor-certification step could convert to zero-token primitives. HONEST CAVEAT on the SEED tier: the recovery comes from the curated<->full FUSION resurfacing deeper curated candidates and re-ranking low-confidence queries — NOT from the ~1M synthetic seed rows. seed-only-useful-recovery is 0.0% for arm C and 0.0% even for the naive un-suppressed union, so on THIS labelled proxy the seed tier adds ~0 labelled discovery. That is a proxy limitation (the suites are written in curated vocabulary), not proof the seeds are useless — re-test with a held-out gold set carrying seed-only intents. It is NOT evidence that more rows hurt. WITHOUT harming the default: on GENERAL queries (curated P@1 1.000 by construction) arm C holds 0.938 and turns a curated WIN into a MISS in only 0.06% of them; the certified-only filter never lowers certified coverage (certified-hit@k 0.939 -> 0.992). The no-generic filter cuts the adversarial generic-card rate 0.008 -> 0.000. (The coverage-GAP slice is EMPTY — the curated index returns >=1 hit for every eval query — so there is no zero-hit gap to recover here.) RECOMMENDATION: keep the curated 112K as the precision-safe default; serve the weighted+diversity full corpus (with these controls) as the discovery/low-confidence lane. Every row stays searchable — nothing is rejected.
