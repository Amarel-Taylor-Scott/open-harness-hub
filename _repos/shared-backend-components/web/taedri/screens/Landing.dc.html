Zero to retrieval in three steps

About sixty seconds on broadband.

01 Sign up with an email Email in, key out. No SSO, by design. Your account secret is shown once and stored only as a hash.
02 Get your key Free plan: 200 requests a day, all seven MCP tools, receipts included. Upgrade only when the meter says so.

Search → edges → compose → receipts

Four deterministic moves between your agent and the corpus.

01 Search

Ranked, scored primitives. Not code dumps.

02 Typed edges

Every primitive declares input → output. Matched by shape, phrasing, purpose: the problem and its solution.

03 Compose

Outcomes become chains of connected edges. Same request, same route.

04 Receipts

Every call leaves a trail you can export. Never raw prompt bodies.

Verified, or experimental.
Never mystery code.

Every result carries one of two labels. Verified means it survived full adversarial validation. Experimental means it works and holds up in most situations while validation finishes. A setting decides how much of the shelf your agents see.

✓ verified Fully adversarially validated. Retrieve with your eyes closed.
experimental Working and dependable for most uses; final validation in progress.
